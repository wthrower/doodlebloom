Flat cel-shaded color illustration, like a modern animated film background. Surfaces read as clearly defined areas of color with crisp, unbroken boundaries where colors meet. No drawn outlines, ink lines, or black keylines anywhere -- shapes are defined by where two colors meet, never by a stroke around them. Densely detailed and intricate: pack the entire composition with distinct objects, layers, ornament and repeating motifs from foreground to background, leaving no large empty or plain areas. Build that density out of MANY separate mid-sized color shapes rather than out of fine texture: keep adding more shapes, not smaller ones. Each individual shape should still be big enough to fill in by hand, so no speckling, stippling, hair-thin slivers, noise or grain. No photographic depth-of-field blur -- everything in the scene is in equally sharp focus.
