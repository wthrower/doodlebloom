Flat cel-shaded color illustration, like a modern animated film background. Surfaces read as clearly defined areas of color with crisp, unbroken boundaries where colors meet. No drawn outlines, ink lines, or black keylines anywhere -- shapes are defined by where two colors meet, never by a stroke around them. The main subjects stand out clearly from what is behind them: give every important object a color and a lightness that contrasts against its immediate background, so foreground reads as separate from background and never blends into it. Vivid, richly saturated color throughout -- clean strong hues rather than muted, dusty, greyed or washed-out ones, but stop short of neon, garish or oversaturated. Densely detailed and intricate: pack the entire composition with distinct objects, layers, ornament and repeating motifs from foreground to background, leaving no large empty or plain areas. Build that density out of MANY separate mid-sized color shapes rather than out of fine texture: keep adding more shapes, not smaller ones. Each individual shape should still be big enough to fill in by hand, so no speckling, stippling, hair-thin slivers, noise or grain. No photographic depth-of-field blur -- everything in the scene is in equally sharp focus.
